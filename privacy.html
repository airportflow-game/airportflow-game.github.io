Local play and storage

Campaign progress, choices, stars, airport discoveries, passenger totals, daily bests, settings and tutorial acknowledgements are stored in the app’s local storage. The play-summary counters record selected gameplay and technical events by puzzle ID. The app does not automatically upload this summary. Its JSON export contains app version, platform and aggregate counts, without names, email addresses, room codes, IP addresses or reconnect tokens. Clear local summary removes the counters and cached export. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes local app data. Android system backups are disabled in the prepared Play build; there is no account or cloud campaign save.

Optional online multiplayer

To run shared races, the app sends game moves, scores, timing, random temporary room/reconnect identifiers and player states to airport-flow-server.onrender.com. The server also processes the connection IP address for connection and abuse-prevention limits. It does not infer location. Other participants see numbered player labels and the intended game progress, points and departures. The app requests no real name or public profile. Room and match state are kept in server memory, with a two-hour maximum room lifetime, twenty-minute idle lobby/result expiry and a 45-second reconnection window; restarting the server removes those rooms. Connection/rate-limit information is transient. Application logs contain aggregate operational counters without raw IPs, reconnect tokens or room codes. Local campaign saves are not uploaded.

Optional daily scoreboard

Global daily rankings are optional. Before joining, you choose an age group stored only on this device. Participation is available from age 13; the under-13 option makes no ranking requests. No date of birth is requested. You can choose a preset pilot avatar and reroll a randomly generated nickname before joining or later in pilot settings. We store the selected avatar index and nickname choice with your pilot and publish them on the rankings; no photo upload or free-text name is requested. Changing these cosmetics preserves your pilot identity, scores and first-attempt limits. Joining creates a random installation credential and a pilot alias, without an email address, password, social login or real name. The credential stays on the device; the game server forwards its hash to the database.

If ranking participation is already enabled and the saved age group is 13 or older, the app makes a technical health connection when it starts to wake the existing game service in the background. Opening daily tasks can repeat this check when readiness is stale; there are no continuous keepalive pings. This health check sends no pilot credential or gameplay result, creates no ranked attempt and starts no attempt timer. The hosting provider and game service necessarily process the connection IP address and technical connection information under the service/security handling described here. Offline-only play and the under-13 choice make no automatic ranking-service startup connection.

We store a random pilot identifier and the first attempt for each UTC day and puzzle tier: start/finish times, outcome, points and elapsed time. The game server temporarily checks the submitted moves against the actual puzzle; move proofs are not saved in the ranking database. Public boards show the chosen pilot alias and preset avatar (older versions use an automatic alias), rank, aggregate score, solved-puzzle count and total time, for the current UTC day, the last 7 days and all time. Other players cannot see the credential or its hash. Offline practice and replays do not improve the ranking.

The existing Render server in Frankfurt communicates with Supabase over HTTPS. The configured Supabase database region is West EU (Ireland). Supabase processes database data on our behalf under its Data Processing Addendum; see its privacy notice for its own account, security and service processing. A European database region does not mean every provider operation occurs only in Europe. Provider terms govern subprocessors and international-transfer safeguards.

Ranked records remain for the chosen all-time service until you delete your pilot. Open Settings → My profile → Delete pilot and scores, then confirm. Older versions may put this action in the ranking settings. After the server confirms deletion, the pilot and all ranked attempts are removed and the device credential is cleared. Local campaign progress is unaffected. Clearing app storage or reinstalling loses the credential and does not itself delete server records; delete the online entry first. There is no cross-device recovery. Ranking processing provides the optional service you request under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; security and prevention of invalid submissions use Article 6(1)(f). You may play the game without joining.

Optional purchases through Google Play

From Android version 1.10, optional additional level packs and pilot/plane designs can be bought through Google Play. The first 100 campaign levels remain free. Each offer explains the content included: individual designs contain one pilot with a matching plane design. A previously purchased design bundle keeps the content originally included. There are no subscriptions and this version has no in-game ads. Availability and prices are provided by Google Play and may vary by country and version.

Google processes checkout, payment and Google-account information under its own terms and privacy policy. Airport Flow does not receive your card or bank details. Through the Google Play Billing Library, the app receives product identifiers, purchase state, purchase identifiers/tokens and signed receipts. The app checks the signed receipt against this app's public Play key before granting content. Verified receipts and design selections are stored locally on your device.

The app queries Google Play to restore purchases and reconcile refunded or revoked items. This version does not send purchase tokens or receipts to the Airport Flow ranking/multiplayer server. Restore uses the same Google Play account. Reconciliation needs a successful Play connection; previously verified content may remain available offline until that connection succeeds. Purchase state is independent of your ranking pilot and is not published on the scoreboard.

Deleting a ranking pilot, clearing app storage or uninstalling does not cancel purchases, refund payments or erase Google's transaction records. Clearing storage removes the local receipt cache; purchases can be restored with the same Google Play account. Refund requests are handled through Google Play. Local purchase processing provides the optional content you request under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; Google's payment processing and retention are governed by its own notices. iOS purchases are not enabled in this release.

Bot practice and local Wi-Fi

Bot practice runs on your device. Local Wi-Fi races communicate directly with the other participating device using ENet. That app-level LAN connection is not encrypted. Internet multiplayer uses certificate-verified WSS/TLS. The app therefore does not claim that every multiplayer connection is encrypted.

Voluntary feedback and support

Send report opens your chosen email application with your written description and diagnostic JSON in the message body. It does not attach a file or send automatically: you review the message and choose Send in your email app. The operator then receives the sender address, email subject/content, any personal information you chose to write and the included play-summary counts. These are used to respond, troubleshoot and improve the game. Sending a report is optional; play does not require it. Please do not include passwords or invitation/reconnect credentials. Email delivery uses your chosen email provider and the support mailbox at Gmail/Google; those providers also process email delivery and security data under their own policies.

Hosting, recipients and international processing

The game service is hosted by Render Services, Inc.; configured service region: Frankfurt (EU Central). Render processes service data under its Data Processing Addendum and maintains some service usage/security information under its own privacy policy. Provider processing can include the United States even when the service region is European. Render’s DPA describes the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and, where it does not apply, standard contractual clauses for relevant transfers. Runtime logs available in our Hobby workspace are retained for 7 days. This period does not describe every separate infrastructure or security record maintained by the provider. The support website is delivered by GitHub Pages, which can process IP address, browser/request information and requested URL for delivery/security; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Gmail/Google and other mail providers may also process data internationally; see Google’s privacy policy. These pages use system fonts and contain no Airport Flow analytics scripts, embedded third-party media or Airport Flow tracking cookies.

Purposes, legal bases and retention

Requested online play is processed to provide the selected game service under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Technical protection and voluntary support/bug analysis use legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f): a functioning, secure service and responding to requests. Optional multiplayer/support data are not required for offline play; without connection data online racing cannot work, and without a return email address support cannot reply. There is no advertising profiling or automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects. Support and feedback messages are kept while the request is being handled and deleted no later than 6 months after it is resolved, unless legal obligations or a specific legal claim require further retention. A deletion request can be made earlier. This is a retention procedure applied by the operator, not an automatic upload or mailbox-cleanup function in the app.

Your rights and deletion

Contact the operator for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection to processing based on legitimate interests, and portability where applicable. You may complain to a data-protection supervisory authority, including the authority in Rhineland-Palatinate. Where processing is based on consent, it can be withdrawn for the future. There is no conventional email/password login. An optional ranking pilot can be deleted as described above; local app data and summaries are separate. Support can handle requests concerning emailed reports and contact providers where required. Rights requests are normally answered within one month; the GDPR allows an extension for complex cases with notification.

Voluntary thank-you voice contributions

Testers may separately email their own short thank-you recording for a discovered destination. We receive the recording, sender address and any supplied destination/permission information via support email. Submission is voluntary, is not recorded through the app microphone, and is not sent to Supabase. We request permission before integrating a recording. Published clips are bundled in the game and audible to its players; no sender name or email address is published by default. For minors, a parent or guardian must submit and authorise the contribution. Processing for review/publication relies on consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw permission by contacting support; we remove the clip from future releases. We cannot recall copies already installed or downloaded. Unselected recordings and ordinary correspondence follow the six-month resolved-support retention period; a selected clip and evidence of permission are retained while it is used, subject to a necessary limited legal-claims retention exception.

Permissions and bundled voices

The game requests network access. It does not request microphone, contacts or location permissions. Spoken greetings, music and images are bundled game assets, not recordings of the player. This release has no advertising SDK, advertising identifier or third-party analytics SDK. Optional Android purchases are described above.

Beim Beitritt entstehen eine zufällige Geräteinstallation-Kennung und ein Pilotenname, ohne E-Mail, Passwort, Social Login oder echten Namen. Die App stellt beim Start eine technische Health-Verbindung her, wenn der Ranglistenbeitritt bereits aktiv und die gespeicherte Altersgruppe mindestens 13 ist. Diese Prüfung sendet weder eine Pilotenzugangkennung noch Spielergebnisse, legt keinen gewerteten Versuch an und startet keine Versuchszeit. Hostinganbieter und Spielserver verarbeiten dabei notwendigerweise die Verbindungs-IP und technische Verbindungsinformationen im hier beschriebenen Rahmen für Betrieb und Sicherheit. Rein lokales Spiel und die Auswahl Unter 13 lösen keine automatische Ranglistenserver-Verbindung beim Start aus.

Gespeichert werden eine zufällige Pilotenkennung und der Erstversuch je UTC-Tag und Schwierigkeitsstufe: Start-/Endzeit, Ergebnis, Punkte und Spieldauer. Der Spielserver prüft die übermittelten Züge vorübergehend am tatsächlichen Rätsel; Zugnachweise werden nicht in der Ranglistendatenbank gespeichert. Öffentlich sind der gewählte Pilotenname und vorgegebene Avatar (ältere Versionen verwenden einen automatischen Namen), Rang, Gesamtpunkte, Anzahl gelöster Rätsel und Gesamtzeit für den aktuellen UTC-Tag, die letzten 7 Tage und die Gesamtzeit.
