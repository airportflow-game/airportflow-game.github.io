Local play and storage

Campaign progress, choices, stars, airport discoveries, passenger totals, daily bests, settings and tutorial acknowledgements are stored in the app’s local storage. The play-summary counters record selected gameplay and technical events by puzzle ID. The app does not automatically upload this summary. Its JSON export contains app version, platform and aggregate counts, without names, email addresses, room codes, IP addresses or reconnect tokens. Clear local summary removes the counters and cached export. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes local app data. Android system backups are disabled in the prepared Play build; there is no account or cloud campaign save.

Optional online multiplayer

To run shared races, the app sends game moves, scores, timing, random temporary room/reconnect identifiers and player states to airport-flow-server.onrender.com. The server also processes the connection IP address for connection and abuse-prevention limits. It does not infer location. Other participants see numbered player labels and the intended game progress, points and departures. The app requests no real name or public profile. Room and match state are kept in server memory, with a two-hour maximum room lifetime, twenty-minute idle lobby/result expiry and a 45-second reconnection window; restarting the server removes those rooms. Connection/rate-limit information is transient. Application logs contain aggregate operational counters without raw IPs, reconnect tokens or room codes. Local campaign saves are not uploaded.

Optional daily scoreboard (from Beta 0.67 when enabled)

Global daily rankings are optional. Before joining, you choose an age group stored only on this device. Participation is available from age 13; the under-13 option makes no ranking requests. No date of birth is requested. From Beta 0.70 you can choose a preset pilot avatar and reroll a randomly generated nickname before joining or later in pilot settings. We store the selected avatar index and nickname choice with your pilot and publish them on the rankings; no photo upload or free-text name is requested. Changing these cosmetics preserves your pilot identity, scores and first-attempt limits. Joining creates a random installation credential and a pilot alias, without an email address, password, social login or real name. The credential stays on the device; the game server forwards its hash to the database.

We store a random pilot identifier and the first attempt for each UTC day and puzzle tier: start/finish times, outcome, points and elapsed time. The game server temporarily checks the submitted moves against the actual puzzle; move proofs are not saved in the ranking database. Public boards show the chosen pilot alias and preset avatar (older versions use an automatic alias), rank, aggregate score, solved-puzzle count and total time, for the last 24 hours, last 7 days and all time. Other players cannot see the credential or its hash. Offline practice and replays do not improve the ranking.

The existing Render server in Frankfurt communicates with Supabase over HTTPS. The configured Supabase database region is West EU (Ireland). Supabase processes database data on our behalf under its Data Processing Addendum; see its privacy notice for its own account, security and service processing. A European database region does not mean every provider operation occurs only in Europe. Provider terms govern subprocessors and international-transfer safeguards.

Ranked records remain for the chosen all-time service until you delete your pilot. From Beta 0.69 open Daily departures → Rankings → the settings icon → Delete pilot and scores, then confirm. Earlier versions offer Delete my entry directly on the scoreboard. After the server confirms deletion, the pilot and all ranked attempts are removed and the device credential is cleared. Local campaign progress is unaffected. Clearing app storage or reinstalling loses the credential and does not itself delete server records; delete the online entry first. There is no cross-device recovery. Ranking processing provides the optional service you request under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; security and prevention of invalid submissions use Article 6(1)(f). You may play the game without joining.

Bot practice and local Wi-Fi

Bot practice runs on your device. Local Wi-Fi races communicate directly with the other participating device using ENet. That app-level LAN connection is not encrypted. Internet multiplayer uses certificate-verified WSS/TLS. The app therefore does not claim that every multiplayer connection is encrypted.

Voluntary feedback and support

Send beta report opens your chosen email application with your written description and diagnostic JSON in the message body. It does not attach a file or send automatically: you review the message and choose Send in your email app. The operator then receives the sender address, email subject/content, any personal information you chose to write and the included play-summary counts. These are used to respond, troubleshoot and improve the game. Sending a report is optional; play does not require it. Please do not include passwords or invitation/reconnect credentials. Email delivery uses your chosen email provider and the support mailbox at Gmail/Google; those providers also process email delivery and security data under their own policies.

Hosting, recipients and international processing

The game service is hosted by Render Services, Inc.; configured service region: Frankfurt (EU Central). Render processes service data under its Data Processing Addendum and maintains some service usage/security information under its own privacy policy. Provider processing can include the United States even when the service region is European. Render’s DPA describes the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and, where it does not apply, standard contractual clauses for relevant transfers. Runtime logs available in our Hobby workspace are retained for 7 days. This period does not describe every separate infrastructure or security record maintained by the provider. The support website is delivered by GitHub Pages, which can process IP address, browser/request information and requested URL for delivery/security; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Gmail/Google and other mail providers may also process data internationally; see Google’s privacy policy. These pages use system fonts and contain no Airport Flow analytics scripts, embedded third-party media or Airport Flow tracking cookies.

Purposes, legal bases and retention

Requested online play is processed to provide the selected game service under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Technical protection and voluntary support/bug analysis use legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f): a functioning, secure service and responding to requests. Optional multiplayer/support data are not required for offline play; without connection data online racing cannot work, and without a return email address support cannot reply. There is no advertising profiling or automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects. Support and feedback messages are kept while the request is being handled and deleted no later than 6 months after it is resolved, unless legal obligations or a specific legal claim require further retention. A deletion request can be made earlier. This is a retention procedure applied by the operator, not an automatic upload or mailbox-cleanup function in the app.

Your rights and deletion

Contact the operator for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection to processing based on legitimate interests, and portability where applicable. You may complain to a data-protection supervisory authority, including the authority in Rhineland-Palatinate. Where processing is based on consent, it can be withdrawn for the future. No game account needs deletion; local app data and summaries can be removed as described above. Support can handle requests concerning emailed reports and contact providers where required. Rights requests are normally answered within one month; the GDPR allows an extension for complex cases with notification.

Voluntary thank-you voice contributions

Testers may separately email their own short thank-you recording for a discovered destination. We receive the recording, sender address and any supplied destination/permission information via support email. Submission is voluntary, is not recorded through the app microphone, and is not sent to Supabase. We request permission before integrating a recording. Published clips are bundled in the game and audible to its players; no sender name or email address is published by default. For minors, a parent or guardian must submit and authorise the contribution. Processing for review/publication relies on consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw permission by contacting support; we remove the clip from future releases. We cannot recall copies already installed or downloaded. Unselected recordings and ordinary correspondence follow the six-month resolved-support retention period; a selected clip and evidence of permission are retained while it is used, subject to a necessary limited legal-claims retention exception.

Permissions and bundled voices

The game requests network access. It does not request microphone, contacts or location permissions. Spoken greetings, music and images are bundled game assets, not recordings of the player. This release has no advertising SDK, advertising identifier, third-party analytics SDK or in-app purchases.

Lokales Spiel und Speicherung

Kampagnenfortschritt, Zugfolgen, Sterne, Flughafenentdeckungen, Passagierzähler, Tagesbestwerte, Einstellungen und bestätigte Spielhinweise liegen im lokalen App-Speicher. Die Spielübersicht zählt ausgewählte Spiel- und Technikereignisse nach Rätsel-ID. Die App lädt sie nicht automatisch hoch. Ihr JSON-Export enthält Appversion, Plattform und zusammengefasste Zähler ohne Namen, E-Mail-Adressen, Raumcodes, IP-Adressen oder Wiederverbindungstokens. Lokale Übersicht löschen entfernt Zähler und zwischengespeicherten Export. Android-App-Speicher löschen oder Deinstallieren entfernt lokale App-Daten. Systembackups sind im vorbereiteten Play-Build deaktiviert. Es gibt kein Spielkonto und keine Cloud-Sicherung der Kampagne.

Freiwilliger Online-Mehrspieler

Für gemeinsame Rennen sendet die App Spielzüge, Punkte, Zeiten, zufällige vorübergehende Raum-/Wiederverbindungskennungen und Spielzustände an airport-flow-server.onrender.com. Der Server verarbeitet zudem die Verbindungs-IP für Verbindungs- und Missbrauchsgrenzen; daraus wird kein Standort abgeleitet. Andere Teilnehmer sehen nummerierte Spielernamen sowie den vorgesehenen Spielfortschritt, Punkte und Abflüge. Die App verlangt keinen Klarnamen und kein öffentliches Profil. Raum-/Spielzustände liegen im Server-Arbeitsspeicher: höchstens zwei Stunden Raumlaufzeit, zwanzig Minuten für inaktive Lobby-/Ergebnisräume und 45 Sekunden Wiederverbindung. Serverneustart entfernt die Räume. Verbindungs-/Begrenzungsdaten sind vorübergehend. Anwendungsprotokolle enthalten zusammengefasste Betriebszähler ohne rohe IPs, Tokens oder Raumcodes. Lokale Kampagnenspielstände werden nicht hochgeladen.

Freiwillige Tages-Rangliste (ab Beta 0.67, wenn aktiviert)

Die globale Tages-Rangliste ist freiwillig. Vor dem Beitritt wählst du eine Altersgruppe, die nur auf diesem Gerät gespeichert wird. Die Teilnahme ist ab 13 verfügbar; bei „Unter 13“ werden keine Ranglistenanfragen gesendet. Ein Geburtsdatum wird nicht abgefragt. Ab Beta 0.70 kannst du vor dem Beitritt und später in der Pilotenverwaltung einen vorgegebenen Avatar auswählen und einen zufälligen Spitznamen mehrfach neu würfeln. Avatarindex und Namensauswahl werden bei deinem Piloten gespeichert und in den Ranglisten angezeigt; es gibt keinen Fotoupload und keine freie Namenseingabe. Änderungen daran erhalten Pilotenkennung, Punkte und Erstversuch-Sperren. Beim Beitritt entstehen eine zufällige Geräteinstallation-Kennung und ein Pilotenname, ohne E-Mail, Passwort, Social Login oder echten Namen. Die Zugangkennung bleibt auf dem Gerät; der Spielserver übermittelt ihren Hash an die Datenbank.

Gespeichert werden eine zufällige Pilotenkennung und der Erstversuch je UTC-Tag und Schwierigkeitsstufe: Start-/Endzeit, Ergebnis, Punkte und Spieldauer. Der Spielserver prüft die übermittelten Züge vorübergehend am tatsächlichen Rätsel; Zugnachweise werden nicht in der Ranglistendatenbank gespeichert. Öffentlich sind der gewählte Pilotenname und vorgegebene Avatar (ältere Versionen verwenden einen automatischen Namen), Rang, Gesamtpunkte, Anzahl gelöster Rätsel und Gesamtzeit für die letzten 24 Stunden, die letzten 7 Tage und die Gesamtzeit. Andere Spieler sehen weder Zugangkennung noch deren Hash. Offline-Training und Wiederholungen verbessern die Rangliste nicht.
